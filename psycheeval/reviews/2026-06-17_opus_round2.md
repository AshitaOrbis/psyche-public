# Publication Review — Round 2 (delta), Opus (steelman)

**Date**: 2026-06-17
**Reviewer**: Opus (general-purpose agent, model opus) — hostile-but-fair steelman
**Document**: gate-driven revision of `reports/psycheeval_v0_3_full_pilot_2026-05-19_v03.md`
**Mode**: delta — read diff + gate_battery_findings.md + gate3_prepare.py + gate3_score.py + full report

**One-sentence verdict**: The reframe is a genuine, well-instrumented insight, but the revision over-promotes a 64-task, single-coder, 2-way attribution result into a load-bearing headline ("generation works") while under-interrogating whether "evaluation vs generation" is even the right cut — and §11 crosses a line the body's own caveats do not support.

## MUST FIX

**M1. [VALIDITY] "Generation works" rests on a task structurally easier than (and asymmetric with) the judging task it's contrasted against — the asymmetry is conceded as a *strength* when it's at least as much a *confound*.** Gate 3 coder (Gemini) vs study judge differ on ≥4 axes simultaneously (has both briefs vs none; 2-way attribution vs quality; coder identity; side-by-side split). Attribution-with-answer-key-visible is far easier; weak behavioral signal + any lexical echo crosses 50%. The report concedes the coder "frequently cites contract phrasing" then argues this "strengthens" the eval-bottleneck reading — it does NOT cleanly: if recovery is surface contract-echo, "generation produces target-distinct text" collapses toward "generation produces contract-distinct formatting" = the shared-formatting null. Downgrade "generation works" → "the conditioned outputs are 2-way *discriminable* by a coder holding both briefs — partly via surface contract-echo." State Gate 3 establishes **discriminability, not adaptation fidelity**. Cut/demote the "if anything, strengthens" move.

**M2. [VALIDITY][CLARITY] "Bottleneck is evaluation, NOT generation" is a false dichotomy, and §11 states it as fact ("persona conditioning works at the generation stage").** Internal contradiction: the report says only target-conditioned judging (v0.4) can tell whether distinctness *serves* the target — you cannot hold that AND "generation works" (which presupposes the answer). §11 "The safest summary is no longer just descriptive" is the single most miscalibrated line: the body added caveats, the conclusion got more confident — wrong direction. Reword to "the **observed judge preferences** are dominated by an evaluation-stage global-pole/structure bias; this does not clear generation of also contributing, because Gate 3 measures discriminability, not target-fit." Keep "evaluation is *a* bottleneck"; drop "*the* bottleneck … NOT generation."

**M3. [VALIDITY] The global-pole-vs-real-quality alternative is never confronted.** Gate 2's "global-pole" (one persona wins regardless of target) is labeled an *evaluation bias*, but the benign reading is unexamined: some personas genuinely elicit better unconditional advice, so an unconditional-quality judge *correctly* prefers it. The report conflates "the judge doesn't measure target-fit" (true by construction — never asked) with "the judge is biased" (not shown). Add: the global-pole pattern is consistent with both an evaluation bias AND a real unconditional-quality gap the design cannot distinguish (no ground-truth quality, no target-conditioned arm). This is the steelman of the opposite conclusion and is currently absent.

## SHOULD FIX

**S1. [SUFFICIENCY] Single-coder, single-model Gate 3 (n=8 per sign-flip cell) is load-bearing for the most-promoted claim.** 64 tasks, one model, one pass, no inter-rater agreement; "both directions ≥0.75" on n=8 = as few as 6/8; dario cell is at the 0.75 floor on the pair that also has the delta-wrinkle. Add a sufficiency caveat at *first promotion* (§1 callout, §4.5), not just buried: "(single-model, single-pass, n=8 per persona; directional discriminability signal, not a measured rate)." §1 currently presents "93% [CI]" as a robust population estimate.

**S2. [VALIDITY][TRANSPARENCY] dario/pawl delta-form contamination understated.** That pair is one of 3 global-pole pairs AND the weakest Gate-3 cell (0.75) AND the brief Gemini saw was *analyst-reconstructed* (C3+delta concat), not the served artifact. Drop it from "all 3 global-pole pairs sign-flip" → "2 of 3 clean pairs sign-flip cleanly; the third (dario/pawl) is confounded by delta-form reconstruction."

**S3. [VALIDITY] Trigger #7 judge/author confound disclosed once then ignored.** Divergence is equally consistent with "gpt-authored contracts are more rewardable" as "opus-as-judge over-rewards." Replace "opus-as-judge effect" (in §1, §4.1, §4.5, §5, §6, §8, §11) with "opus-judge/gpt-author cell" / "judge-family-OR-author-family effect." Honest claim: "not judge-unanimous — concentrated in one judge×author cell, not attributable to the judge side under this scope."

**S4. [CLARITY] "Matching survives surface control" and "matching is global-pole" are the SAME content effect described positively then negatively.** Reader may think matching has two independent endorsements. Add a clause: what survives in Gate 1 (a content, non-formatting preference) IS the signal Gate 2 identifies as global-pole — not a second, independent endorsement of target-matching.

**S5. [TRANSPARENCY] Post-hoc/exploratory status well-flagged at §4.5 but under-propagated to §5 ("reclassified") and §8 (FIRES).** A predeclared trigger is being scored fired by a post-hoc mediator-laden regression. Prepend "(post-hoc, exploratory)" to each §5 "reclassified" bullet; in §8 #8 note firing is established by a post-hoc mediator-laden regression, consistent with but not a clean causal confirmation.

**S6. [VALIDITY] Mediator logic direction asserted without justification for the specificity downgrade.** Controlling a mediator is conservative for effects that *survive* but anti-conservative for *nulls* (over-control can manufacture a null). If specificity causes more target-apt structure/length, conditioning on structure/length removes the legitimate pathway. "Specificity IS a surface effect" → "specificity's increment is **not separable from** the formatting it induces."

## NICE TO HAVE

**N1. [VALIDITY] 93% CI [0.872,0.963] treats n=128 coupled judgments as independent** (64 tasks × 2 coupled outputs). Cluster/task-level CI would be wider; precision overstated.
**N2. [CLARITY] "25% of pairs" = ~16 tasks; state n inline** so "25%" isn't read as a stable rate.
**N3. [CONTRIBUTION] The downgrades are honest and §9 is strong; miscalibration is localized to the Gate-3 promotion** (§1 callout, §4.5 synthesis, §11). Surgically deflate only the four "generation works"/"works at the generation stage"/"safest summary no longer descriptive" promotions — over-claiming and burying-the-finding are the same edit here; the true finding is more persuasive stated modestly.

## Adversarial bottom line
**Defensible core** (survives scrutiny): forced-choice unconditional judging rewards a global pole + contract scaffolding; conditioned outputs are nonetheless 2-way discriminable by a briefed coder; therefore target-conditioned judging is the right v0.4 priority.
**Over-reach** (MUST-FIX): "generation works / works at the generation stage / bottleneck is evaluation NOT generation / safest summary no longer descriptive." Highest-value edit: demote §11 to "the conditioned outputs are blind-discriminable; whether that discriminability is target-*serving* is the v0.4 question."
