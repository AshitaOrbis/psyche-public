## MUST FIX

1. [VALIDITY] **"Isolates a genuine public-anchor contribution distinct from narrative form" overstates.** C5_CONTRACT vs C5_NONPUBLIC_CONTRACT does not hold the contract constant in *content* (contract content derives from different packets). The varying thing is (packet + packet-dependent contract), not packet alone. Downgrade "isolates ... distinct from narrative form" → "consistent with a public-anchor contribution; residual human-authorship style confound the design cannot rule out (§9)". Touches claim (c). [§1¶3, §4.2]
2. [VALIDITY] **The ladder cannot "localize the benefit to contract-first ordering."** Single-path ladder, two *not-detected* steps, and the D5 equivalence test was deliberately skipped — so the premise "L0→L1 and L1→L2 contribute nothing" is unestablished by the report's own admission. L2→L3 (69.7%) is confounded with position-in-ladder / accumulated structure / interactions. Reword in all three places (§1, §4.3, §11): "contract-first ordering is the only single-step addition that reaches detection in this one ladder ordering; whether ordering is causally privileged vs. merely the threshold-crossing point is not separable in a single-path design." The single biggest over-reading. Touches claim (d).
3. [TRANSPARENCY/VALIDITY] **"Mechanism" language is used in §4.3/§11 while trigger #8 (reward-hacking covariate regression) is an admitted TODO that §8 says must clear "before any 'mechanism' wording is finalized."** Internal-coherence failure. Either run the regression first (sign-off gates on it — good) or strip "mechanism" → "preference-pattern" until it runs. Touches all claims, esp. (d).

## SHOULD FIX
