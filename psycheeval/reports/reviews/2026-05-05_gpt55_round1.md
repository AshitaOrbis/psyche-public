   The live scenario file has **48 scenarios across 8 families, including omitted `ambition_status`**.
   - Claim-alignment: Undermines (d) and (f), especially scenario-family claims about C5/PAE.

2. **Scalar sample-size wording is numerically wrong** — [VALIDITY]

   "C0–C4 rows cover … (432 outputs × 3 judges)." Those are not output counts.
   Actual outputs are C5 = 72; C0–C4 = 144 each. Cross-provider scalar records are

   §6 reports all-judge same-author decisive rates, e.g. C0 vs C4 = 0.237.
   §14a reports cross-provider same-author pooled rates, e.g. C0 vs C4 = 0.260.
   These are not validation duplicates. Cross-provider results also change marginal
   calls: C1 vs C5 CI straddles 0.5, unlike §6.
   - Claim-alignment: Affects (b), (c), and (d).

   - Claim-alignment: Affects all pairwise-supported claims (a)–(d).

   Text says: "GPT-5.5 judging GPT-5.4 outputs produced a larger same-provider halo…"

   to cross-provider judges. Numbers match §14b, but interpretation is reversed.
   - Claim-alignment: Directly undermines (e).

   Fix taxonomy count and explain null/undefined κ handling.
