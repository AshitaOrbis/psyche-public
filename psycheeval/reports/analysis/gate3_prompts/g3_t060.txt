“People are too shallow” is usually not a useful first diagnosis. It may be true of some readers, but it can also be a clean way to avoid asking whether the work is under-signaled, structurally loose, or asking for effort without giving enough return.

A practical test:

- Ask where they got pulled in, where they got lost, and what felt intentional versus merely obscure.

What the patterns mean:

- If readers misunderstand in different ways, the piece probably lacks control or orientation.

Ambition is not a defense by itself. A hard piece still needs to create footholds: structure, pressure, specificity, movement, consequences. If those are present, readers can do serious work with you. If they are absent, “they didn’t get it” does not mean much.
