It feels honest because it stings, but the sting isn't evidence — it's the character-frame doing its job, which is to convert a specific repeated pattern into a fixed identity and then close the file. The thing worth looking at is the pattern. "Failing to finish" covers at least four different failure modes, and they don't share a fix:

1. **Never really starting** — the project lives as a plan; the first ugly draft never happens.
2. **Starting strong, abandoning around 20–40%** — novelty wears off, the middle gets boring, attention moves on.

4. **Finishing in private and never shipping** — done but undelivered, because shipping makes it real and judgeable.

From the outside they all look identical ("I didn't finish"). From the inside they all feel identical ("I'm bad at this"). They're different mechanisms with different costs: 2 is appetite collapse, 3 is decision avoidance, 4 is exposure avoidance, 1 is something else again.
